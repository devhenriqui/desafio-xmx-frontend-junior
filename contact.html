Contact Tenurima Support

We're here to help you reach your health goals and assist with any order inquiries.

Dear Customer,

We are the official Support Team of Tenurima. This guide will help you contact us quickly and efficiently so we can resolve your request as fast as possible.

Official Support Channel: support@tenurima.com

Our dedicated customer support agents are available 24/7 to assist you.

1. Open your email client

Open your preferred email provider or app (Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail, Yahoo, etc.).

2. Compose message to our official email

Click "Compose" or "New Email" and enter:

  • To: support@tenurima.com

(This is our official support channel and guaranteed communication method.)

3. Fill in the "Subject" line clearly

In the subject line, include your issue category and your order number:

  • Refund Request – Order #12345
  • Tracking / Delivery Inquiry – Order #98765
  • Address Change – Order #33221
  • General Product Question – Order #55678

Providing your order number helps us look up your account instantly.

4. Include necessary details in the message body

For the fastest resolution, please ensure your message includes:

  • Your full name (as used during checkout)
  • The email address associated with your order
  • A clear explanation of how we can help
  • Any relevant photos, screenshots, or receipt attachments

5. Send your request

Review your information and click Send. Our team typically responds within 24 business hours.

💡 Important Tip

The more details and clear order references you provide in your initial message, the quicker our support agents can process your request without requiring back-and-forth clarification emails.
